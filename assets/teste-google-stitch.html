Clear, authoritative insights into the financial requirements for Canada's primary economic immigration pathways.

speed

Express Entry (Federal)

The primary system for skilled workers. Requires significant proof of funds for individuals and families to establish themselves in major Canadian hubs.

Base Requirement

~$13,757 CAD / 1 Person

waves

Atlantic Immigration

Designed for skilled workers and international graduates wanting to live in one of Canada's four Atlantic provinces. Lower financial thresholds apply.

Base Requirement

~$3,503 CAD / 1 Person

landscape

Rural & Northern Pilot

Community-driven program designed to spread economic benefits to smaller communities. Settlement funds vary by community size but follow federal patterns.

Base Requirement

~$9,200 CAD / 1 Person
